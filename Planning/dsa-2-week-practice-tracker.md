# 2-Week DSA Practice Tracker

This tracker starts after your first notes batch. The point now is pattern
recognition under light time pressure, not passive re-reading.

## Mentor Rule: Spiral Review

Moving on does not mean dropping older topics.

- Every new topic block must include at least one revisit to an older topic.
- Once a topic is introduced, it stays in rotation until it feels automatic.
- Review days are not filler days. They exist to keep earlier patterns sharp.
- If an older pattern starts slipping, it comes back into the next practice block.

## Challenge Flow Rule

- Challenges are populated per session, not as one permanent pile.
- Once a challenge is completed and reviewed, move it into `challenges_exercises/Archive`.
- Active challenge folders should only contain the current working set.
- Archived challenges remain available for future review and spiral practice.

## Daily Standard

- 2 timed problems
- 1 untimed problem with full spoken explanation
- Write time and space complexity for every solution
- Log every miss or hesitation in the mistakes log
- Spend 10 to 15 minutes reviewing why the pattern fit

## Exit Criteria After 2 Weeks

You are ready to move on when most of the following are true:

- easy problems are clean inside 10 to 15 minutes
- you can name the likely pattern before writing code
- you can explain brute force before optimized
- your mistakes log shows fewer repeated errors
- you can talk through edge cases without freezing

## What happens after this block

When you move to the next topic, keep one of these in every study week:

- 1 old-topic retry day
- 1 mixed set that includes at least one earlier pattern
- 1 re-explanation from memory of a previously solved problem

The rule is simple: not everything will be new. Some time is always reserved for
retention.
